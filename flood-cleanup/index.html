Floods start in many ways: heavy rain, storm surge, a river over its banks, a dead sump pump, a sewer backup or a major plumbing failure, and all of them call for fast professional help. Expect gray water (Category 2) or black water (Category 3) in almost any flood, and with it bacteria, contamination and potentially hazardous substances that threaten your health. Day or night, 24/7, Palm Bay Restoration sends flood cleanup crews throughout Palm Bay to remove floodwater, sanitize the affected rooms and safely restore your home or building.

The first job is getting the water out quickly, and our submersible and truck-mounted pumps are able to clear thousands of gallons every hour. After that, IICRC-certified technicians on our team measure how contaminated the area is, then sanitize it with antimicrobial products, HEPA-filtered air cleaning and targeted disinfection of all surfaces the water reached. We tear out and properly dispose of what cannot be saved, such as soaked carpet padding, contaminated drywall and ruined insulation.

Once the space is extracted and sanitized, our team brings in commercial drying gear, namely low-grain refrigerant (LGR) dehumidifiers plus high-velocity air movers, so the structural components dry all the way through. Our crew returns daily, using thermal imaging and calibrated meters to track moisture until the structure is completely dry and mold growth is prevented. From start to finish we document each step for your insurance claim, with the moisture maps, equipment logs, photos and scope-of-work reports adjusters ask for.

Why Professional Flood Cleanup Matters

Flood cleanup is far more than pumping out standing water. Chemicals, bacteria, sewage and other contamination ride in with the flood and create real health dangers. Protecting your family from E. coli, hepatitis and similar waterborne illness takes proper containment, correct use of protective equipment (PPE) and proven sanitizing methods, all things professional teams train for.

Improper cleanup also tends to miss moisture you cannot see, and within days that trapped water can cause a major mold problem. Professional technicians use moisture meters, thermal imaging and psychrometric calculations to make sure every affected area, including wall cavities, subfloors and framing, reaches an acceptable drying standard. Drying by measurement is the one dependable method for avoiding secondary damage, which can add thousands to the repair bill.

Signs It Is Time for Flood Cleanup

Catching these issues early helps you avoid costly problems and protect your property. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Standing water anywhere on your property, whatever the source
  • Roof or wall openings left by a storm, with rain pouring through
  • A sewer or drain backup pushing contaminated water into living spaces
  • Seepage at door thresholds, window wells or cracks in the foundation
  • Water collecting along the foundation because the yard is saturated
  • Flooring that squelches when you walk on it and walls damp to the touch
  • Water flooding out of a dishwasher, water heater or washing machine

Maintenance Tips for Flood Cleanup

A little prevention goes a long way in avoiding expensive restoration emergencies. Follow these tips from our IICRC-certified technicians:

  • A backwater valve on your sewer line keeps a backup from pushing sewage indoors
  • Store valuables and important papers off the basement floor in waterproof containers
  • Look into a flood insurance policy, including when your home sits outside a mapped flood zone
  • Roof runoff should leave the downspout far enough out that it cannot soak the foundation
  • Cracks in the foundation should be sealed and the basement walls given a waterproof coating
  • Check that grading on all sides of the house carries water away from the foundation
